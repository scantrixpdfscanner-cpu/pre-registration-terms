Pre-registration Reward Terms

Last updated: May 4, 2026

By pre-registering for Scantrix PDF Scanner on Google Play, eligible users may receive a one-time reward of 7 days of premium access.

Eligibility

  • Available only to users who pre-register through Google Play in eligible regions.
  • Reward is tied to the Google account used for pre-registration.
  • One reward per eligible user.

Reward Details

  • Reward: 7 days of premium access in the app.
  • Reward is delivered by Google Play after app launch or when the reward campaign is active.
  • Requires installing and opening the app on an Android device.
  • No cash value, non-transferable, not for resale.

Limitations

  • If you already have premium access, the reward may not extend beyond your existing premium period.
  • Availability and timing are subject to Google Play systems.

Changes

We may modify or discontinue this reward at any time to comply with platform rules or policy updates.
